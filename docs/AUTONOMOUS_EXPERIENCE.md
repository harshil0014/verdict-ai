# Autonomous investigation experience

Evidence submission starts document reading, safe public-reference acquisition, private local address comparison, investigation and next-source preparation. New evidence and witness responses trigger reassessment. Users inspect outcomes and supporting records; ordinary research does not require a human-review form.

## Address privacy and accuracy

- Account Settings contains the affirmative external-address disclosure and consent. The choice applies to existing and future eligible investigations until changed or revoked. No permission is enabled by this release.
- Account revocation stops future lookups even for older case permissions. Area-only consent cannot be expanded by an older address-level case setting. Stricter case restrictions remain effective.
- Authorization is checked before a shared provider slot and again after pacing immediately before HTTP. Revocation cannot recall already transmitted requests. Previously preserved evidence is retained.
- Without external consent, source descriptions are compared locally. A shared PIN or approximate proximity does not make Lakeview Apartments and Sunrise Residency the same destination. Differing units and named landmarks remain meaningful contradictions.
- Multiline addresses and escaped line breaks in OCR output retain exact original source quotes and hashes. Empty headings and instructions to verify an address are not addresses. Building suffixes such as “Apartments” are preserved.
- The ordinary case shows a short conclusion and supporting-evidence links. Maps, coordinates, provider estimates and integrity diagnostics remain under advanced details. A destination contradiction does not prove physical delivery, non-delivery or personal receipt.

## Evidence acquisition and authorized actions

Each completed investigation persists an acquisition plan identifying the useful question, recipient, rationale, priority and prior request outcome. This happens without opening a review dialog. Existing open, answered, unavailable or withdrawn requests retain their history and do not produce duplicate invitations from the same suggestion.

“Share request” creates the existing private response link directly. It sends no message. The owner must authorize disclosure by sharing it with the intended recipient. The link expires, accepts a bounded response and remains revocable. Manual composition is optional; no witness response or contact is invented.

PayPal Sandbox resolution retains the separate bounded merchant policy and durable dispatch, amount, freshness, idempotency and provider-confirmation safeguards. This release neither enables a policy nor sends a financial action. Real-money merchant onboarding remains unavailable.
